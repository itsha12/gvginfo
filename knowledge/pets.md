# Pets

Source: Guild Wars Wiki, pulled 2026-10-08. Lines starting **Tested** are in-game test results and override the wiki.
Ranger animal companions.

- Level 20: 80 armor. Health 480 (Elder), 540 Hearty, 510 Playful, 450 Aggressive, 420 Dire.
- Damage at 12 Beast Mastery (non-crit): Elder 17–29, Dire 20–32, Hearty 15–25. Crit ≈ ×1.414 (not max damage).
- Attack every 2s (Racing Beetle and Moa are faster). Most pets move 25% faster than normal.
- In GvG pets take 15% death penalty when they die, and your skills are disabled for 10..3 seconds (Beast Mastery
  0..15).
- Pet attack skills only work within party range.
