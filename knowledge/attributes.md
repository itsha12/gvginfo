# Attributes and runes

Source: Guild Wars Wiki, pulled 2026-10-08. Lines starting **Tested** are my own in-game results and override the wiki.

## Attribute points
- 200 points at level 20. Cumulative cost per rank: 1:1, 2:3, 3:6, 4:10, 5:15, 6:21, 7:28, 8:37, 9:48, 10:61, 11:77,
  12:97. 12 is the highest you can buy.
- Common spreads: 12+12+3; 12+10+8 (+5 left); 12+11+6; 12+9+9 (+7 left); 11+10+10 (+1 left).
- Secondary profession: no access to its primary attribute, and runes and headgear only work for your primary
  profession, so secondary attributes cap at 12.
- Rank cap is 20 (21 with weapon "+1 attribute (chance)" mods).

## Bonus ranks
- Attribute runes: Minor +1, Major +2 (−35 Health), Superior +3 (−75 Health). Rune bonuses don't stack with each other
  but do stack with headgear (+1). Primary profession only.
- Weapon suffix "of <Attribute>" and the off-hand inscription "Master of My Domain": +1 with a 10–20% chance, only
  for the skill being used.
- Weakness lowers all attributes above 0 by 1 (see Conditions).

## Primary attributes (inherent effects)
- **Strength** (Warrior): attack skills gain 1% armor penetration per rank (not auto-attacks; not if the skill already
  has armor penetration).
- **Expertise** (Ranger): −4% energy cost per rank for attack skills, rituals, touch skills and all Ranger skills.
- **Divine Favor** (Monk): Monk spells that target an ally heal the primary target +3.2 per rank (rounded); untargeted
  Monk spells heal the caster. Applies after the spell's own effect.
- **Soul Reaping** (Necromancer): +1 energy per rank when a non-spirit creature dies within about 2508 gwinches;
  at most 3 times per 15 seconds.
- **Fast Casting** (Mesmer): spells cast in time / 2^(rank/15) (about ×0.955 per rank); signets −3% per rank. In PvP
  it does not affect non-Mesmer spells or signets with activation under 2 seconds, and gives no recharge benefit.
  Not subject to the −25% cast-time cap.
- **Energy Storage** (Elementalist): +3 max energy per rank.
- **Critical Strikes** (Assassin): +1% critical chance per rank; energy per critical hit: 1 at ranks 3–7, 2 at 8–12,
  3 at 13–17, 4 at 18+.
- **Spawning Power** (Ritualist): +4% Health per rank to creatures you create (spirits, minions); +4% weapon spell
  duration per rank.
- **Leadership** (Paragon): +2 energy per ally affected by your shouts and chants, max 1 energy per 2 ranks.
- **Mysticism** (Dervish): −4% cost per rank for Dervish enchantments (the armor bonus is PvE only).
