# Health and regeneration

Source: Guild Wars Wiki, pulled 2026-10-08. Lines starting **Tested** are my own in-game results and override the wiki.

## Max Health (level 20, PvP)
- Base max Health 480.
- Rune of Superior Vigor +50 (doesn't stack with another Vigor rune). Rune of Vitae +10 each (stacks).
- Survivor Insignia: +15 chest, +10 legs, +5 on each other piece.
- Attribute runes cost Health: Minor +1 (no cost), Major +2 (−35 Health), Superior +3 (−75 Health). The Health
  penalties always stack.
- Most Health possible from runes and insignia: 610 (Dervish 635).
- Martial weapon suffix "of Fortitude" +30 Health. Shields and foci: up to +30 Health, or +45 while enchanted or in a
  stance, or +60 while hexed (from handles and cores).

## Regeneration and degeneration
- Each pip = 2 Health per second. Net regeneration is capped at +10 pips and degeneration at −10 pips; the cap is
  applied after adding everything up.
- Regeneration is not healing, and degeneration is not damage.
- Natural regeneration (while not attacking, not losing Health, not targeted by or using foe-targeted spells or
  signets): starts at +1 pip after about 5 seconds, then +1 pip every 2 seconds, up to +7.

## Healing
- Healing reductions stack multiplicatively; the total is capped at −40%.
- "Health gain" and life stealing are not healing: healing modifiers and Deep Wound don't affect them, and they don't
  trigger on-heal effects.
- Life stealing ignores armor and is not damage (but see the Master of Damage page: it does count it).

## Sacrifice
- Sacrifice is Health loss as a percentage of max Health. It can kill you, and nothing reduces it except lowering
  your max Health.
