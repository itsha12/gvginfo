# Adrenaline

Source: Guild Wars Wiki, pulled 2026-10-08. Lines starting **Tested** are my own in-game results and override the wiki.

- 1 strike (25 units) per successful weapon hit (per hit for multi-hit attacks).
- 1 unit per 1% of max Health lost to damage.
- Each adrenal skill fills its own pool. Using an adrenal skill empties its pool and removes 1 strike from every other
  adrenal skill.
- Recharging or disabled adrenal skills can't build adrenaline.
- All adrenaline is lost on death or after 25 seconds without gaining any.
- Adrenaline-gain multipliers cap at +100%; flat adrenaline gains are uncapped.
- Blocked attacks give no adrenaline. Furious weapons: 10% chance to double adrenaline gained on hit.
