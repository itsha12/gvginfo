# Casting, recharge and interrupts

Source: Guild Wars Wiki, pulled 2026-10-08. Lines starting **Tested** are my own in-game results and override the wiki.

## Casting and aftercast
- Most spells, signets, glyphs, chants, echoes and rituals: stated cast time + ¾s aftercast (you can't move, attack
  or act during it).
- No activation and no aftercast: shouts, stances, flash enchantments. These can be used while acting or knocked down,
  except flash enchantments, which can't be used while activating another skill or knocked down.
- Activation-time modifiers (non-attack) cap at −25% and +150%. Fast Casting is outside that cap.
- Dazed doubles spell cast time.

## Recharge
- Recharge starts when the skill finishes or is interrupted. Failed skills recharge instantly.
- Recharge reductions stack multiplicatively to −50% (a single skill effect can go further).
- Effects that change recharge round to the nearest second.

## Interrupts, knockdowns and failing
- Interrupts stop any skill with an activation time and all attacks; the interrupted skill goes on full recharge.
- Knockdown (2s by default; 3s with Stonefist and some skills) is not an interrupt, so anti-interrupt effects don't
  stop it.
- A skill fails if a requirement isn't met, the target dies, or a failure effect triggers: costs are lost and the
  skill recharges instantly. Low-attribute skills that say so have about a 50% failure chance.
