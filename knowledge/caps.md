# Stacking caps

Source: Guild Wars Wiki, pulled 2026-10-08. Lines starting **Tested** are my own in-game results and override the wiki.

| Property | Max bonus | Max penalty | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Attack speed | +33% | −50% | multiplicative |
| Movement speed | +34% | −50% | a few skills go past it (Burning Speed, Pious Haste, Dash; Winter's Embrace below) |
| Block / miss chance | 100% | — | multiplicative |
| Healing | — | −40% | multiplicative |
| Adrenaline gain | +100% | −50% | |
| Health / energy regeneration | +10 pips | −10 pips | also for single effects |
| Recharge time | — | −50% | a single skill effect can go past it |
| Activation time | +150% | −25% | Fast Casting exempt |
| Armor (bonus) | +25 | down to 60 | see Armor |
| Attributes | 20 | 0 | 21 with weapon chance mods |

Most effects of the same skill don't stack (the newest or strongest wins). Different skills' effects stack unless
capped.
