# Guild halls and Flux

Source: Guild Wars Wiki, pulled 2026-10-08. Lines starting **Tested** are in-game test results and override the wiki.

- Isle of Meditation: largest hall. The Obelisk flag stand on the upper path behind gates makes the beach statues fire
  Fireballs at the team not holding it. Miasma on the north path (+5 degeneration pips, can't be removed, spreads to
  adjacent creatures).
- Each hall's own wiki page lists its specific mechanics. Add a section here per hall as you learn them.
- The monthly Flux changes the rules (gvg.report shows the current one, e.g. Minion Apocalypse in October 2026).
