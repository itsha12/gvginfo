# Energy

Source: Guild Wars Wiki, pulled 2026-10-08. Lines starting **Tested** are in-game test results and override the wiki.

## Base energy by profession (from profession armor)
| Profession | Max energy | Energy regeneration (pips) |
|---|---|---|
| Warrior | 20 | 2 |
| Ranger | 25 | 3 |
| Assassin, Dervish | 25 | 4 |
| Paragon | 30 | 2 |
| Monk, Necromancer, Mesmer, Elementalist, Ritualist | 30 | 4 |

One pip = 1 energy every 3 seconds.

## Adding energy
- Energy Storage (Elementalist primary): +3 max energy per rank.
- Radiant Insignia: +3 chest, +2 legs, +1 other pieces. Rune of Attunement: +2 each (stacks).
- Staff: +10 inherent (+5 more from a prefix). Focus items: up to +12.
- Martial weapon inscription "I have the power!": +5 energy.
- Zealous weapon prefix: +1 energy per hit, −1 energy regeneration.
- Caster inscriptions and cores with "+15 energy, −1 energy regeneration" (see Weapon modifiers).

## Limits
- Energy regeneration and degeneration cap at ±10 pips.
- Overcast lowers max energy, recovers 1 per 3 seconds, and persists through death.
- Each maintained enchantment costs −1 energy pip (see Blocking, stances and enchantments).
