# Blocking, stances and enchantments

Source: Guild Wars Wiki, pulled 2026-10-08. Lines starting **Tested** are my own in-game results and override the wiki.

## Block and miss
- Block and miss chances stack multiplicatively (two 50% effects = 75%). Miss is checked before block.
- Blocked attacks deal no damage and give no adrenaline. "Evade" no longer exists (it is now block).

## One at a time
- Only one stance at a time: a new one replaces the old (the old one's end effect triggers, if any).
- Likewise one preparation, glyph, weapon spell and form at a time, and one bundle (e.g. item-spell ashes).

## Enchantments and hexes
- Reapplying: if the new cast would be weaker, the stronger values continue until the original would have expired; if
  equal or stronger, it acts as newly cast.
- Removal usually takes the most recent one ("covering").
- Upkeep: each maintained enchantment costs −1 energy pip. It drops if net energy degeneration would pass −10, if
  energy hits 0, or if the target is out of party range (5020) for more than 5 seconds.
