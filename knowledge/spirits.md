# Spirits

Source: Guild Wars Wiki, pulled 2026-10-08. Lines starting **Tested** are in-game test results and override the wiki.
Nature rituals and binding rituals.

- Health ≈ 20 × level, +4% per Spawning Power rank of the creator (level 10: 200 Health at 0 Spawning Power, 296 at
  12; level 20: 400 / 592).
- Armor ≈ 2 + 6 × level (level 10: 62, level 15: 92, level 20: 122). Level is stated in each ritual's description and
  scales with attribute.
- Attack spirits: ranged attacks every 2s (2s activation), longbow range (1498), armor-ignoring damage. They can crit,
  but crits do no extra damage. 31 energy, 4 pips.
- Immune to enchantments, hexes, other spirits' passive effects, weapon spells, wards, healing (Divine Favor
  included), and every condition except Burning. "Health gain" effects work on them.
- Can be knocked down. Affected by shouts and chants unless the skill says otherwise.
- Allies, not party members. Non-fleshy.
- A same-name allied spirit inside spirit range replaces the old one (triggering end effects); nature rituals also
  replace enemy spirits of the same name.
